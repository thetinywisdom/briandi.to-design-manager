Variations

Fig 2. Two hands

Four studies of the same pair, from Michelangelo’s Creation of Adam. The hands are level, and the fingertips almost meet. Move across a plate and the dots step aside. They spring back when you leave. On a touch screen, drag.

A. Halftone

A regular grid. The size of each dot follows the shading, as in a newspaper photograph.

B. Contour stipple

Dots along the edges and the creases, with a light stipple in the shadows.

C. Character

A density ramp, one monospace character to a cell. Darker skin uses a heavier mark.

D. Two-tone depth

Each hand is its own field of dots. A small spark sits in the gap, and the hands lean toward it.
